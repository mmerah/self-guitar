Box 4 sits at fret 12, and box 5 sits under box 1 at frets 2–5. After box 5 the pattern starts again with box 1. Goal: both boxes up and down in 8ths at 84 BPM.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: box 4, saying the roots aloud.
- 2 minutes: box 5 at frets 2–5, then box 1 straight after it.
- 5 minutes: the exercise from 64 BPM.
- Review: one pass of boxes 2 and 3.

## Box 4

Box 4 starts on E at fret 12 on string 6. Strings 4 and 3 use the ring on fret 14; string 2 shifts up a fret, to 13 and 15.

::fretboard[Box 4: fingers]{scale="minor-pentatonic" root="A" box="4" labels="fingers"}

## Box 5

Box 5 starts on G. It sounds at frets 14–17, but on an acoustic guitar it's easier to play an octave lower, at frets 2–5: the same notes, the same shape. Its top edge is box 1's bottom edge at fret 5.

::fretboard[Box 5: intervals]{scale="minor-pentatonic" root="A" box="5" labels="intervals"}

## The whole neck

Put the five boxes side by side and they tile the neck: each box's top edge is the next box's bottom edge.

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Losing the root. Before each box, find the A on string 6 or 5 and say it.
- Playing box 4 on the wrong side of fret 12. The index starts at 12, not 11.
- Pressing too hard high on the neck: strings there need very little pressure.

:::

**Mastery check:** three clean passes in a row of boxes 4 and 5 at 84 BPM.
