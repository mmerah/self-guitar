A strummed chord sounds clean when the strings that don't belong to it stay silent, and a blues groove needs chords that stop exactly when you want. This lesson gives both hands a muting job.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: strum D, A and C hard and wide on purpose, adjusting the mute until no wrong bass note sounds.
- 2 minutes: chokes on E, one strum per beat, silence on every "&".
- 3 minutes: the exercise, from 46 BPM up to 70.
- Review: 1 minute of C–G changes.

## Damping unwanted bass strings

On A and C the low E must not sound; on D, neither the low E nor the A string. Aiming the strum helps, but a mute is safer:

- **C:** let the tip of the ring finger (on fret 3 of the A string) lean lightly against the low E. It touches the string without pressing it, so it can't ring.
- **A and D:** let the fretting-hand thumb peek over the top of the neck and rest lightly on the low E.
- **D:** the A string still needs care. Start the strum at the D string; if your thumb reaches the A string too, let it touch that as well.

::chords{ids="D,A,C"}

Test it: strum all six strings. A muted string gives a soft "tick" with no pitch; a ringing wrong string means the mute missed.

## Chokes

A choke stops a chord right after you strum it. Strum, then instantly **release the pressure** of the fretting fingers without lifting them off the strings, and let them flatten a little so they also touch the open strings. Whatever still rings, the side of the strumming hand stops on its way back up.

```atex
\tempo 70
:8 (0.6 2.5 2.4 1.3 0.2 0.1){ch "E"} r (0.6 2.5 2.4 1.3 0.2 0.1) r (0.6 2.5 2.4 1.3 0.2 0.1) r (0.6 2.5 2.4 1.3 0.2 0.1) r
```

Short, punchy chords like this are a blues and rock-'n'-roll staple. You'll use them on E7, A7 and B7 in the 12-bar lessons.

## Practise

::exercise{id="muted-strums-d-a-c"}

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Pressing with the muting thumb or fingertip, so a wrong note sounds instead of a tick.
- Lifting the fingers right off the strings for a choke. The open strings then ring on.
- Choking late. The silence should land exactly on the "&".

:::

**Mastery check:** a clean pass of the exercise at 70 BPM.
