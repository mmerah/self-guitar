A 6/8 bar has six quick notes that fall into two big swaying beats. Goal: the D D U D D U ballad strum on G, Em, C and D at 84 BPM, and the same feel picked as an arpeggio.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: count "1 2 3 4 5 6" out loud with the metronome below, leaning on 1 and 4.
- 2 minutes: the three patterns on an Em chord, one minute each.
- 4 minutes: D D U D D U on the ladder, from 56 BPM.
- 2 minutes: the p-i-m arpeggio, from 56 BPM.
- Review: one verse of House of the Rising Sun.

## Two big beats

In 6/8 you count six 8th notes, but you feel **two** beats: 1 and 4. Each big beat splits in three, which gives the rolling, rocking sound of ballads and sea songs.

Compare 3/4: the same six 8th notes, grouped as three beats of two ("1 & 2 & 3 &"). In 6/8 the groups are "**1** 2 3 **4** 5 6".

::tool[6/8 click: accent on 1 and 4]{name="metronome" bpm=120 ts="6/8"}

## Three patterns

Count every bar "1 2 3 4 5 6". Each pattern plays on Em with a click on every 8th.

**1. The two big beats:** down on 1 and 4, let the chord ring.

::strum{pattern="D - - D - -" bpm=120 chord="Em" ts="6/8"}

**2. The lilt:** D - U D - U. A down on 1, an up on 3; the same again on 4 and 6.

::strum{pattern="D - U D - U" bpm=120 chord="Em" ts="6/8"}

**3. The ballad strum:** D D U D D U, the lilt with an extra down on 2 and 5. The hand comes up silently after each first down.

::strum{pattern="D D U D D U" bpm=120 chord="Em" ts="6/8"}

::exercise{id="six-eight-strum-g-em-c-d"}

## Arpeggiated 6/8

Pick the same chords one note at a time: the thumb takes a bass note on each big beat, then i and m answer on strings 3 and 2. Two groups of three, every bar.

::exercise{id="six-eight-arpeggio-g-em-c-d"}

## Songs in 6/8

House of the Rising Sun is six even notes a bar, picked. The Skye Boat Song and Rio Grande use the lilt; When Johnny Comes Marching Home marches on the two big beats.

::song[House of the Rising Sun: 6/8 arpeggio]{id="house-of-the-rising-sun" tempo=90}

::song[Skye Boat Song: the lilt in G]{id="skye-boat-song" tempo=80}

::song[When Johnny Comes Marching Home: A minor]{id="when-johnny-comes-marching-home" tempo=80}

::song[Rio Grande: a 6/8 shanty in G]{id="rio-grande" tempo=80}

::tip[Sway on 1 and 4. If your body rocks three times a bar, you're playing 3/4.]

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Accenting 1, 3 and 5. That's 3/4; the accents in 6/8 are 1 and 4 only.
- Rushing the up-strums. All six 8th notes are the same length.
- Reading the ladder's BPM as 8th notes. It counts quarter notes: at 84 BPM the big beats go by at 56.

:::

**Mastery check:** three clean passes in a row of D D U D D U on G, Em, C and D at 84 BPM.
