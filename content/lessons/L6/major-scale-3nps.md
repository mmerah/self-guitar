With three notes on every string, the picking hand gets an even pattern and the fretting hand slides up the neck as it goes. Goal: two octaves and a bit of G major in triplets at 90 BPM.

## How to practise this

- 1 minute: one string at a time, three notes, slowly, saying the finger numbers.
- 5 minutes: the exercise from 58 BPM up to 90.
- 2 minutes: the same run starting from A (fret 5): it's position 2 of the same pattern.
- Review: one pass of the three-position exercise.

::fretboard[G major, 3 notes per string, position 1]{scale="major" root="G" box="1" labels="fingers"}

Strings 6 to 3 use fingers 1, 2, 4: frets 3-5-7 on strings 6 and 5, then 4-5-7 on strings 4 and 3. Strings 2 and 1 are a whole step then a half step (frets 5-7-8): fingers 1, 3, 4. The hand moves up a fret onto string 4 and another onto string 2.

Count "1-trip-let 2-trip-let": each string is one beat. Alternate pick down-up-down, up-down-up, so every beat starts with the opposite stroke.
