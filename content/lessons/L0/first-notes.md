Time for the fretting hand. You'll play six notes on the two thinnest strings, using just two fingers: **index on fret 1** and **ring on fret 3**.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: each note of the first tab, slowly; fix any buzz.
- 5 minutes: the exercise at the bottom.

## The notes

On the **B string** (string 2): open is B, fret 1 is C, fret 3 is D.

On the **high e string** (string 1): open is E, fret 1 is F, fret 3 is G.

```atex
\tempo 60
:4 0.2 1.2 3.2 r | 0.1 1.1 3.1 r
```

## Making a clean note

1. Press with the fingertip, right behind the fret wire, not on top of it.
2. Press only as hard as you need for a clear note. Buzzing means a little closer to the fret, not harder.
3. Keep fingers you're not using hovering close to the strings.

::tip[Leave your index finger down on fret 1 while the ring finger plays fret 3. It saves a movement and steadies the hand.]
