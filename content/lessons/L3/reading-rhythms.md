The stems under the tab and the shapes on the staff say how long each note lasts. Goal: read a 4-bar melody with 8ths, a dot, a rest and a triplet, at 80 BPM.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: clap each bar of the exercise while counting "1 & 2 &" aloud.
- 5 minutes: the exercise from 52 BPM up to 80, counting under your breath.
- Review: one pass of the notes on strings 1–3.

## How long is each note?

- **Whole note** (open oval): 4 beats. **Half note** (open, with a stem): 2 beats.
- **Quarter note** (filled, with a stem): 1 beat. **8th note** (one flag or beam): half a beat, counted "1 &".
- **16th note** (two flags or beams): a quarter of a beat, counted "1 e & a".
- A **dot** adds half the value: a dotted quarter lasts 1½ beats, so the 8th after it lands on an "&".
- A **tie** joins two notes of the same pitch into one sound.

Every value has a rest of the same length: silence, but you keep counting.

## Triplets

A **triplet** squeezes three notes into the time of two: three 8ths in one beat, marked with a 3. Count "1-trip-let", evenly, with no gap before the next beat.

::warning[Don't rush the 8th after a dot. Say "1 & 2 &" and play it exactly on the "&".]
