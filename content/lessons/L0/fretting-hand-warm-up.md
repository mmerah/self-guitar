A short drill that wakes up all four fretting fingers, teaches them to work one at a time and builds calluses. Use it as the warm-up for every session from here on.

## How to practise this

- 1 minute: the drill on the low E string only, very slowly, watching the pinky.
- 3 minutes: the exercise below.
- From now on: 2 minutes of this drill to start every session.

## The drill: 1-2-3-4

::exercise{id="chromatic-warm-up"}

Keep each finger down until the next string; on the way back, all four start down and lift one at a time. The pinky will feel weak: this drill is the cure.

## Sore fingertips and calluses

For two or three weeks the fingertips will sting, until hard skin (calluses) forms. Short daily sessions build calluses best. If a fingertip blisters, stop for the day.

## Is the guitar easy to play?

If fret 1 takes a lot of force, the strings may sit too high (the **action**). A guitar shop can lower it cheaply: the best upgrade for a beginner's guitar.

:::tip
Ten to twenty minutes a day beats an hour once a week. Leave the guitar out of its case so it's easy to pick up.
:::

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Pressing hard with all four fingers at once. Press only as hard as the note needs.
- Fingers flying far off the strings between notes. Keep them hovering close.
- Rushing. The tempo ladder only counts clean passes.

:::

**Mastery check:** a clean pass of the warm-up at 60 BPM.
