Notes played by the fretting hand alone sound smooth and vocal: this is legato. Goal: hammer-ons, pull-offs and slides in box 1, in time at 80 BPM.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: one hammer-on and one pull-off per string, slowly, at equal volume.
- 5 minutes: the exercise from 58 BPM up to 80.
- 1 minute: improvise in box 1 using only legato after each picked note.
- Review: one pass of box 1 at 80.

## Hammer-on

Pick the lower note, then bring a finger down hard on the higher fret, like a little hammer, right behind the fret wire. The note sounds without a second pick. In tab it's marked with an arc.

```atex
\tempo 60
:4 5.3{lf 2 h} 7.3{lf 4} 5.2{lf 2 h} 8.2{lf 5}
```

## Pull-off

The reverse: both fingers down, pick the higher note, then pull the top finger **sideways** off the string, plucking it as you go. The lower finger must already be in place.

```atex
\tempo 60
:4 7.3{lf 4 h} 5.3{lf 2} 8.2{lf 5 h} 5.2{lf 2}
```

## Slide

Pick, keep the pressure, glide to the new fret, don't pick again. Slides join boxes, and they sound like a voice sliding into a note.

::exercise{id="hammer-pull-slide-box-1"}

::tip[The hammered or pulled note must be as loud as the picked one. If it's quieter, aim for the fret, not the string.]

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Pulling off straight up: the note dies. Pull towards the floor, plucking the string.
- Hammering with a flat finger. Use the tip, fast and from close by.
- Slides that rush: the arrival note belongs on its beat.

:::

**Mastery check:** three clean passes in a row of the legato exercise at 80 BPM.
