The shuffle you played in E moves to A, and the last two bars get a turnaround: a short line that leads back to the top. Goal: the 12-bar shuffle in A with its turnaround at 84 BPM.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: the A bar alone, swung, until the long-short feel runs by itself.
- 1 minute: bars 11 and 12 on their own, slowly.
- 5 minutes: the exercise from 58 BPM up to 84.
- Review: one pass of the boogie shuffle in E.

## Swung 8ths

A shuffle plays each pair of 8ths **long-short**, like a triplet with the middle note left out. Count "**1**-trip-**let** **2**-trip-**let**" and play only on the number and the "let". Say "**hump**-ty **dump**-ty" along with the playback.

::tool[Metronome with triplets]{name="metronome" bpm=70 sub=3}

## The shuffle in A

It's the E-boogie move on new strings. Open root, plus the next string at fret 2 (index), then fret 4 (ring):

- **A bars:** open string 5, with string 4.
- **D bars:** open string 4, with string 3.
- **E bars:** open string 6, with string 5.

## The turnaround

The last two bars of a 12-bar turn the song round. Here, bar 11 walks down string 4, fret 5, 4, 3, 2 (G, F♯, F, E), over the open A. That line lands on E, and bar 12 is E7, the V chord that pulls back to A.

```atex
\tempo 70
\tf t8
:4 (0.5 5.4{lf 5}) (0.5 4.4{lf 4}) (0.5 3.4{lf 3}) (0.5 2.4{lf 2}) | :2 (0.6 2.5 0.4 1.3 0.2 0.1) :8 (0.6 2.5) (0.6 2.5) (0.6 4.5) (0.6 4.5)
```

On the very last chorus, end with a single A7 instead of bar 12.

::exercise{id="shuffle-in-a-turnaround"}

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Swinging the A bars but playing the turnaround straight. The whole chorus shuffles.
- Letting string 6 ring on A and D bars. Rest the picking hand lightly on it.
- Stretching the ring finger with a stiff wrist. Bring the thumb lower behind the neck.

:::

**Mastery check:** three clean passes in a row of the A shuffle with its turnaround at 84 BPM.
