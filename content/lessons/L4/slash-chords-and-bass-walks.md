Change one bass note and a plain chord change becomes a melody in the low strings. Goal: walk the bass down from G to A under seven chords at 80 BPM.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: the three slash moves below, slowly, listening only to the bass.
- 4 minutes: the descending bass from G, from 52 BPM.
- 2 minutes: D, D/C, G/B, A7 or the walking fills, alternating days.
- Review: 1 minute of boom-chick on G, C, D and Em.

## What the slash means

**G/F♯** reads "G over F♯": play a G chord, but put F♯ in the bass. The letter before the slash is the chord, the one after it is the lowest note. Your strum barely changes; the bass line moves.

## G, G/F♯, Em

Hold G with the middle finger on string 6. For G/F♯ the index takes string 6 at fret 2, and its tip touches string 5 to mute it. The top strings don't move.

::chords{ids="G@3-2-0-0-0-3,G/F#,Em"}

The bass steps G, F♯, E: three neighbouring notes of the G major scale.

## C, C/B, Am

From C, move the ring finger from fret 3 to fret 2 on string 5: that's C/B. Then the ring finger hops to string 3 and the open A rings out for Am. The bass goes C, B, A.

::chords{ids="C,C/B,Am"}

## D, D/C, G/B

Finger D with index, middle and pinky; the ring finger is then free to add C at fret 3 of string 5. For G/B, play G without string 6: index on B (string 5, fret 2), ring on string 1.

::chords{ids="D,D/C,G/B"}

D/C pulls hard towards G/B: the C wants to fall to B. Listen for it.

## The full walk down

Put the moves together and the bass walks down a whole G major scale: G, F♯, E, D, C, B, A. Pick each bass note, then strum only the top three strings so the bass stays clear.

::chords{ids="G@3-2-0-0-0-3,Em,D,C,Am"}

## Walking fills

You can also walk between ordinary chords. Fill the last two beats of a bar with the scale notes that lead, step by step, to the next root: G A B into C, C B A into G.

::exercise{id="walking-bass-g-c-d"}

Then add the same fills to a real song: Red River Valley is G, C and D throughout.

::song[Red River Valley: add walks between G, C and D]{id="red-river-valley" tempo=80}

::tip[Walk only into the important changes, such as the end of a line. A walk in every bar gets busy.]

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Letting string 5 ring under G/F♯. The A clashes: mute it with the index tip.
- Strumming all six strings. The bass note is the point; strum the top three.
- Rushing the walking notes. They are steady quarter notes, in time with the strums.

:::

**Mastery check:** three clean passes in a row of the descending bass from G at 80 BPM.
