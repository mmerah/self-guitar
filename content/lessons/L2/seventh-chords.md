A seventh chord is a major chord plus one more note, the seventh, which gives it a restless, bluesy pull. E7, A7 and B7 are the three chords of a blues in E.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: build B7 finger by finger and pick each string. Fix any muted string.
- 1 minute of E7–B7 changes, using the anchor finger.
- 1 minute of A7–E7 changes.
- Review: 1 minute of Am–Dm changes.

## E7 and A7: take a finger off

In the open E and A chords some notes appear twice. To make the seventh chord, one of the doubled root notes is swapped for the seventh.

**E7** is E with the ring finger lifted: **middle** on fret 2 of the A string, **index** on fret 1 of the G string. The D string now rings open: that D is the seventh, and it replaces the E (a root) that the ring finger was playing.

::chords{ids="E,E7"}

**A7** is A with the middle finger lifted from the G string: the **index** stays on fret 2 of the D string and the **ring** on fret 2 of the B string. The open G is the seventh, replacing the A (a root) that was on fret 2 of the G string. The diagram shows A7 with middle and ring; either fingering works, but staying in the A shape makes A to A7 a one-finger move.

::chords{ids="A,A7"}

## B7

Four fingers: **middle** on fret 2 of the A string (5), **index** on fret 1 of the D string (4), **ring** on fret 2 of the G string (3), **pinky** on fret 2 of the high e (1). The B string rings open. Strum from string 5.

::chord{id="B7"}

## The B7 ↔ E7 anchor

In both chords the **middle finger** sits on fret 2 of the A string. Keep it down and move only the others. That's the change at the end of every 12-bar blues in E.

::tool[E7 to B7 changes]{name="changes" a="E7" b="B7"}

::tool[A7 to E7 changes]{name="changes" a="A7" b="E7"}

::strum{pattern="D - D U D - D U" bpm=70 chord="B7"}

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- On B7, the ring finger touching the open B string. Keep it on its tip.
- Strumming the low E on B7 or A7.
- Lifting the anchor middle finger between B7 and E7.

:::

**Mastery check:** 40 changes in one minute between E7 and B7.
