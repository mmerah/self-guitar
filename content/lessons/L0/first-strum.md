Em is the friendliest chord on the guitar: two fingers, and every string rings. The goal is even down-strums in time with a click.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: finger Em and pick each string; fix any dull one.
- 2 minutes: down-strums with the strum player at 60.
- 5 minutes: the exercise at the bottom.

## Em

Middle finger on fret 2 of the A string (5), ring finger on fret 2 of the D string (4). Everything else is open.

::chord{id="e-m"}

Strum down slowly across all six strings and listen. If a string sounds dull, check that no finger is leaning on it.

## Down-strums in time

Each arrow is one strum on the beat. On each dash your hand moves back up without touching the strings. Press play and strum with the highlighted arrow.

::strum{pattern="D - D - D - D -" bpm=60 chord="e-m"}

:::tip
Strum from the elbow and wrist together, like shaking water off your hand. Keep the motion going even through the dashes: that steady swing is your built-in clock.
:::

## A taste of what's next

Up-strums catch only the thinner strings. Try it slowly; it returns in Level 2.

::strum{pattern="D - D U - U D U" bpm=60 chord="e-m"}

## Practice

Aim for even, relaxed strums at 70 BPM.
