A solo guitar arrangement of a folk tune needs only two things: the melody on top and a bass note under it on the strong beats. Play a simple one, then two traditional tunes solo.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: the Amazing Grace melody alone (the library version), singing along.
- 4 minutes: the arrangement below from 52 BPM.
- 3 minutes: one traditional tune from the library, one section a day.
- Review: the p-i-m-a arpeggio, once at 90.

## Melody and bass, the simple version

The thumb plays the chord's root on beat 1, together with the melody note, and lets it ring. The fingers play the rest of the tune on top. That's all: no fills yet.

::song[Amazing Grace, the melody]{id="amazing-grace" tempo=80}

This first phrase is in C, so every bass note (C, F, G) is fretted. Keys like D, A and E (and G) put the roots on open strings and free the fretting hand; in Level 9 you will arrange this tune yourself, in D.

::exercise{id="amazing-grace-melody-bass"}

::warning[The melody must stay the loudest voice. If the bass covers it, play the thumb softer, not the fingers harder.]
