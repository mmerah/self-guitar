Bm is the A-shape minor at fret 2, and it turns up in nearly every song in D or G. Goal: 40 changes a minute between D and Bm.

## How to practise this

- 1 minute: Bm picked string by string.
- 2 minutes: 1-minute changes, D to Bm and G to Bm.
- 4 minutes: the exercise from 52 BPM up to 80.
- Review: 1 minute of C to F.

## Bm

Index across fret 2 from string 5; **middle** on fret 3 of string 2; **ring** and **pinky** on fret 4 of strings 4 and 3. String 6 stays silent.

::chord{id="Bm" voicing="x-2-4-4-3-2"}

## Faster barre changes

- **Shape first:** the three fingers form the chord in the air while the hand travels; the index lies down last.
- **Lighten, don't lift:** between two barres (Bm to F♯m, F to G), release the pressure, keep the fingers touching the strings, slide.
- **Look ahead:** your eyes go to the next fret a beat before the change.
- **Open-string trick:** on the last "&" before the change, strum open strings while the hand moves. Nobody notices.

## In a song

The Parting Glass uses D, G, A and Bm.

::song[The Parting Glass]{id="the-parting-glass" tempo=80}

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Strumming string 6 on Bm. Start on string 5 and mute string 6 with the index tip.
- The middle finger on string 2 muting string 1. Arch it.
- Stopping to fix every buzz during 1-minute changes. Keep moving: a fair try counts.

:::

**Mastery check:** 40 changes in one minute between D and Bm.
