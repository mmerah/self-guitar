The same five notes, A C D E G, cover the whole neck in five boxes. Boxes 2 and 3 sit just above box 1. Goal: both boxes up and down in 8ths at 84 BPM.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: box 2 slowly, saying the note names on string 6 and string 1.
- 2 minutes: box 3 the same way.
- 5 minutes: the exercise from 64 BPM, +4 after three clean passes.
- Review: one pass of box 1 at 80.

## Box 2

Box 2 starts on C, the next scale note after the root on string 6. On every string, box 2's lower note is box 1's higher one: the two boxes overlap.

::fretboard[Box 2: fingers]{scale="minor-pentatonic" root="A" box="2" labels="fingers"}

The roots (squares) are on string 4 at fret 7 and string 2 at fret 10.

## Box 3

Box 3 starts on D at fret 10: index at 10, ring at 12, pinky at 13 on string 2. On string 3 the index reaches back to 9 and the pinky takes 12.

::fretboard[Box 3: intervals]{scale="minor-pentatonic" root="A" box="3" labels="intervals"}

Roots: string 5 at fret 12 and string 2 at fret 10.

::exercise{id="a-minor-pentatonic-boxes-2-3"}

::tip[Find each box from the roots first: put a finger on every square, then fill in the other notes.]

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Playing box 2 with box 1's fingering. In box 2 the pinky takes fret 10 on most strings.
- Forgetting the step back on string 3 in box 3 (fret 9, not 10).
- Rushing at the top of the box. The turn-around note gets the same length as the others.

:::

**Mastery check:** three clean passes in a row of boxes 2 and 3 at 84 BPM.
