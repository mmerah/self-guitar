This is the all-purpose strumming pattern of folk and pop: the eighth-note motion from Level 1 with two strums left out. The goal is to play it for two minutes at 80 BPM without stopping.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: the pattern on one chord (G) at 60, saying "down, down-up, up-down-up".
- 4 minutes: the exercise, from 52 BPM up to 80.
- 2 minutes: a nonstop run at your best clean tempo with the metronome. When the ladder reaches 80, make it two full minutes at 80.
- Review: 1 minute of the eighth-note exercise from Level 1.

## The pattern

Count "1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &". Strum where there's a letter, and swing past the strings where there's a dash:

- **1**: down
- **&**: _miss_ (hand goes up, no strum)
- **2 &**: down, up
- **3**: _miss_ (hand goes down, no strum)
- **& 4 &**: up, down, up

::strum{pattern="D - D U - U D U" bpm=60 chord="G"}

Beat 3 is the tricky bit: the hand moves down **past** the strings without touching them, then catches the up-strum on "3 &".

## Practise

G, Em, C, D with the pattern on each bar.

::exercise{id="d-du-udu-groove"}

## Two minutes, nonstop

A pattern is only yours when it runs on autopilot. If you make a mistake during the nonstop run, keep the hand moving and rejoin on the next beat.

::tool[Metronome at 80]{name="metronome" bpm=80 sub=2}

::tip[Level 2 is mostly strumming. You can mix in the first fingerstyle lessons of Level 3 (p-i-m-a and the notes on strings 1–3) on alternate days: they only need Level 1 chords.]

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Stopping the hand on the missed strums. That's what throws the timing off.
- Strumming on beat 3. After "2 &" the hand keeps moving down without touching the strings; the next sound is the up-strum on "3 &".
- Changing the chord late. Use the last up-strum (the "&" of 4) as your open-string moment.

:::

**Mastery check:** a clean pass of the exercise at 80 BPM. Then make the two-minute nonstop run part of your practice until it feels easy.
