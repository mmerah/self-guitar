A guitar slips out of tune between sessions, so tuning comes first every time you pick it up. The built-in tuner listens through your microphone.

## How to practise this

- Tune all six strings with the tuner, low to high, then check them all once more.
- Do it at the start of every session from now on. After a week it takes under a minute.

## Standard tuning

From the thickest string to the thinnest: **E A D G B E**. A way to remember it: _Eager Ants Dig Gardens By Evening_.

::tool{name="tuner" tuning="standard"}

## How to tune a string

1. Pluck one open string and let it ring.
2. Watch the needle. Below the target, **tighten** the tuner peg a little; above it, **loosen**.
3. Turn slowly and pluck again. Stop when the reading stays within **±5 cents** and turns green.

:::tip
Always finish by tuning _up_ to the note. If you overshoot, go a little below and come back up: the string holds its pitch better.
:::

Check all six strings, then check them once more. Tightening one string pulls the neck slightly, which can nudge the others.

::warning[Turn the peg in small steps. A string tuned far too high can snap.]

Mastery check: tune all six strings to within ±5 cents using the tuner.
