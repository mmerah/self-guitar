In a chord melody the tune is the top note and the harmony hangs underneath it, all on one guitar. Goal: the first phrase of Ode to Joy in G, melody on string 1 with triads under it, at 80 BPM.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: the melody alone on string 1. Sing along.
- 2 minutes: the four triad grips below, each one picked and then strummed lightly.
- 5 minutes: the chord melody on the ladder, from 52 BPM.
- Review: the C, Am, F, G triads from [Triads on string sets](/learn/triads-on-string-sets).

## Step 1: the melody on one string

Ode to Joy (Beethoven, 1824) is in the public domain. In G its first phrase runs B B C D, D C B A, G G A B, B A A. On string 1 that is frets 7, 8, 10, 5 and 3.

```atex
\tempo 80
\ts (4 4)
:4 7.1{lf 2} 7.1{lf 2} 8.1{lf 3} 10.1{lf 5} | 10.1{lf 5} 8.1{lf 3} 7.1{lf 2} 5.1{lf 2} | 3.1{lf 2} 3.1{lf 2} 5.1{lf 4} 7.1{lf 2} | 7.1{lf 2}{d} 5.1{lf 2}.8 5.1{lf 2}.2
```

One string makes the shape of the tune easy to see: every step up the scale is a step up the neck.

## Step 2: a triad under each note

On the strong beats, play a triad on strings 1–3 whose **top note is the melody note**. Only two chords are needed, G and D, but each in the inversion that puts the right note on top:

- **B on top:** G as D G B, frets 7-8-7.
- **D on top:** D as F♯ A D, frets 11-10-10.
- **G on top:** G as B D G, frets 4-3-3.
- **A on top:** D as D F♯ A, frets 7-7-5.

::fretboard[G major on strings 1–3]{triads="major" root="G" strings="1-3"}

::fretboard[D major on strings 1–3]{triads="major" root="D" strings="1-3"}

## Step 3: put them together

The triad lands on beat 1 of each bar; the notes in between are melody alone. In the last bar the A is held over D to close the phrase.

::exercise{id="ode-to-joy-chord-melody"}

::tip[Let the top note sing: pick string 1 a little harder than the two strings under it.]

## Where this goes next

This is the core of solo arranging: melody on top, harmony below. In Level 9 you add a bass line under it and fills between phrases, starting with [the melody on top](/learn/arrange-melody-on-top).

::song[Ode to Joy: the full melody in C]{id="ode-to-joy" tempo=100}

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- A triad whose top note isn't the melody. Check the string-1 fret before you choose the grip.
- Letting the melody drop out between chords. Keep the line legato as you shift.
- Strumming hard. A chord melody is played softly, the melody one step louder.

:::

**Mastery check:** three clean passes in a row of the Ode to Joy chord melody at 80 BPM.
