"Drunken Sailor" is a traditional sea shanty with only two chords, Em and D. It's your first complete song: one line of words per bar.

## How to practise this

- 1 minute of Em–D changes.
- 4 minutes of the exercise, from 46 BPM up to 70. Say the words in rhythm while you strum.
- Then play the whole song from the library, verse and chorus.
- Review: 1 minute of A–D changes.

## The song

Each line is one bar of four down-strums. In the last line the chord changes halfway: two strums of D on "early in the", two of Em on "morning".

- **Em** What shall we do with a drunken sailor,
- **D** What shall we do with a drunken sailor,
- **Em** What shall we do with a drunken sailor,
- **D** Early in the **Em** morning?

The chorus ("Way hay, and up she rises…") uses exactly the same four bars.

## Play along

::exercise{id="drunken-sailor-strum"}

When the loop is steady, play the full song: [Drunken Sailor](/play/drunken-sailor).

:::tip
Em to D is a hand move with no shared fingers. Use the open-string strum on beat 4 of each Em bar to buy time.
:::

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Strumming all six strings on D. Start from the D string.
- Missing the half-bar change in the last line. Count "1 2 **3** 4": Em arrives on 3.
- Slowing down for the change. The strumming hand keeps the beat.

:::

**Mastery check:** a clean pass of the Drunken Sailor exercise at 70 BPM.
