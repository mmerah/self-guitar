The barre chords in this level are named by their note on string 6 or 5. Start with string 6: learn the seven natural notes there, then find each one in a flash.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: play the notes up and down, saying each name.
- 3 minutes: quiz rounds below, until one takes under 30 s.
- Tomorrow: one quiz round before anything else.
- Review: one pass of the first-position notes on strings 4–6.

::fretboard{notes="naturals" strings="6" frets="0-12" labels="notes"}

Landmarks: E open and at 12, A at 5, C at 8. B–C and E–F are one fret apart; every other pair is two.

::exercise{id="notes-low-e-string"}

## Quiz

The quiz asks each note once: tap its fret on string 6. Fret 12 counts as open.

::fretboard{quiz="string-6"}

::warning[Don't count frets from the nut every time: jump from the nearest landmark (0, 5, 8 or 12).]

**Mastery check:** a quiz round on string 6 in 30 seconds or less, at most one miss.
