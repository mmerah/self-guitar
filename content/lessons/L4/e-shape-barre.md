A barre chord is an open shape moved up the neck, with the index finger doing the job of the nut. Goal: an E-shape barre where all six strings ring, and the change from E to F♯ at 70 BPM.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: the stepping stones below, string by string, at fret 5.
- 5 minutes: the exercise from 46 BPM, +4 after three clean passes, up to 70.
- 1 minute: hold the barre, pick each string, release. Repeat five times.
- Review: 1 minute of C to mini F changes.

## The idea

Play E with your **middle, ring and pinky** instead of the usual fingers. Your index is now free. Slide the whole shape up one fret and lay the index flat across all six strings at fret 1: that's **F**. Up three frets it's **G**, up five it's **A**.

::chord{id="F" voicing="1-3-3-2-1-1"}

The chord is named after the note under the index on string 6. Fret 1 is F, fret 3 is G, fret 5 is A.

::chord{id="G" voicing="3-5-5-4-3-3"}

## Stepping stones

Don't fight all six strings on day one. Build the barre in steps, at **fret 5** first: the strings sit lower there and need less pressure than at fret 1.

1. Index alone across fret 5. Pick each string: aim for six clear notes.
2. Add the middle on fret 6 of string 3. Pick strings 1–3 only: the top half of the A chord.
3. Add ring and pinky on fret 7 of strings 5 and 4. Now all six.
4. When fret 5 rings, try fret 3 (G), then fret 1 (F).

::chord{id="A" voicing="5-7-7-6-5-5"}

## Pressure, not squeeze

- **Index:** straight, close to the fret wire, rolled a little onto its bony outer edge. The soft crease between joints mutes strings.
- **Thumb:** low on the back of the neck, roughly behind the middle finger. Not hooked over the top.
- **Pull, don't pinch:** let the weight of the arm pull the fingers into the strings, elbow slightly in. Clamping with the thumb tires you in seconds.
- **Just enough:** press until the buzz stops, then ease off a little. You need less than you think.

::exercise{id="e-to-f-sharp-barre"}

::tip[In the exercise, keep the 2-3-4 shape in your hand while it moves: only the index changes, lying down on F♯ and lifting on E.]

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- A muted string 3 or 2: a crease of the index sits on it. Slide the index a few millimetres up or down.
- The thumb squeezing hard. Rest after 30 seconds of barre; pain means too much grip.
- Index too far from the fret wire, so you need twice the pressure.

:::

**Mastery check:** three clean passes in a row of E to F♯ at 70 BPM.
