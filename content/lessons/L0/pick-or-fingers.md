You can play an acoustic guitar with a pick or with your bare fingers. You'll use both: a pick for strumming, the fingers for fingerstyle from Level 3.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: single open strings with the pick, slowly. Then with the thumb.
- 5 minutes: the exercise below.

## Holding a pick

Rest the pick on the side of your curled index finger, pointing at the strings. Press your thumb flat on top, so only the tip of the pick shows. Hold it firmly enough that it doesn't spin, loosely enough that your hand stays soft.

::tip[A medium pick (around 0.6-0.7 mm) is a good all-rounder for strumming and single notes.]

## Playing with the fingers

Without a pick, the **thumb** plays the three bass strings (6, 5, 4) and the fingers take the treble strings. For now, use the side of your thumb and let it rest on the next string after each note.

## Your first rhythm

Only the picking hand plays; the fretting hand can rest on your leg.

::exercise{id="open-string-pulse"}

The tempo ladder speeds up after three clean passes in a row and steps back when you miss.

::tool{name="metronome" bpm=60}

:::warning
"Clean" means every note rings, on the beat, with no extra string sounding. Speed comes later.
:::
