"Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star" is a traditional tune that everybody can hum, so you'll hear straight away if your chords line up with the melody. It uses only G, C and D, with changes every two beats.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: only the A phrase (bars 1–4) until the G–C–G changes are easy.
- 4 minutes: the whole exercise from 46 BPM. Changes every two beats are a step up, so don't rush the ladder.
- Hum or sing the tune while you strum. It tells you where the changes fall.
- Review: 1 minute of C–G changes.

## The form

The song has two four-bar phrases (16 beats each), A and B, played as **A B A**. In most bars the chord changes halfway, so you strum each chord twice.

- **A:** **G** Twinkle, twinkle, **C** little **G** star, **C** how I **G** wonder **D** what you **G** are.
- **B:** **G** Up a- **C** bove the **G** world so **D** high, **G** like a **C** diamond **G** in the **D** sky.

The chord name sits just before the syllable where it changes.

::chords{ids="G,C,D"}

## Play along

The exercise is phrase A then B. For the full song, finish with A again.

::exercise{id="twinkle-in-g-strum"}

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Changing chords late because the two-strum chords feel rushed. Use the open-string strum on the second strum before a change.
- Losing your place in the form. Say "A, B, A" to yourself.
- Strumming harder on the changes. Keep every strum the same volume.

:::

**Mastery check:** a clean pass of the Twinkle exercise at 70 BPM.
