The classic blues rhythm riff: two strings at a time, a finger rocking between two frets, swung. You'll play it over the same 12-bar form you just strummed.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: the E riff alone, slowly, until the index–ring rocking is easy.
- 4 minutes: the 12-bar exercise, from 52 BPM up to 80, counting the bars.
- Review: 1 minute of the strummed shuffle in E from the last lesson.

## The E riff: E5 and E6

Pick the open low E and the A string together, with down-strokes only. On the A string, alternate between **fret 2 (index)** and **fret 4 (ring)**, two strokes each:

```atex
\tempo 70
\tf t8
:8 (0.6 2.5) (0.6 2.5) (0.6 4.5) (0.6 4.5) (0.6 2.5) (0.6 2.5) (0.6 4.5) (0.6 4.5)
```

Fret 2 gives E5 (E and its fifth, B); fret 4 gives E6 (E and C sharp). Keep the index down while the ring finger plays fret 4.

## A and B7

- **A bars:** the same shape one string thinner: open A string plus the D string at frets 2 and 4. The low E must stay silent, so let the fretting thumb rest on it, as in the muting lesson.
- **B7 bars:** hold the B7 chord and use two of its notes. The middle finger (B, string 5) stays; pair it with the index on fret 1 of the D string (D sharp), then with the ring on fret 2 of the G string (A). No stretch needed.

::chord{id="B7"}

## The swing

The exercise plays the riff swung, long-short, like the strummed shuffle: it's your reference for the feel. Listen to one pass first.

::exercise{id="boogie-shuffle-e"}

For a heavier, punchier sound, rest the edge of the strumming hand lightly on the strings right next to the bridge. This is a **palm mute**: the notes still sound, but short and thick.

When it flows, play the full arrangement: [12-Bar Blues Shuffle in E](/play/blues-in-e-shuffle).

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Playing straight eighths. Say "**hump**-ty **dump**-ty" and make each pair long-short.
- Letting the low E ring through the A bars. Mute it with the thumb.
- Lifting the index for fret 4. Leave it down; only the ring finger moves.

:::

**Mastery check:** a clean pass of the boogie exercise at 80 BPM.
