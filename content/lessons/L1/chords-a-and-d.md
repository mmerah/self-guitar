A and D are two bright major chords that sit in the same corner of the neck. With E they make the three main chords of the key of A.

## How to practise this

- 2 minutes: pick through A and D string by string; fix any dull string.
- 1 minute of A–D changes, then 1 minute of D–E.
- 3 minutes of the A–D exercise from 46 BPM; when it reaches 70, switch to the A–D–E exercise.
- Review: 1 minute of Em–Am changes.

## A

Three fingers in a row on fret 2: **index** on the D string (4), **middle** on the G string (3), **ring** on the B string (2). Strum from the open A string (5); the low E stays silent.

::chord{id="A"}

Three fingers in one fret is a squeeze. Angle them slightly so each fingertip has its own spot, and let the thin high e string ring open under your ring finger.

## D

A small triangle on the three thinnest strings: **index** on fret 2 of the G string (3), **ring** on fret 3 of the B string (2), **middle** on fret 2 of the high e (1). Strum only the four thinnest strings, from the open D string.

::chord{id="D"}

## Check every string

Before you strum, pick each string of the chord one at a time, from thick to thin. A dull "thunk" means a finger is touching that string: arch the finger more or move it closer to the fret wire.

## Changes

Before each change, "see" the next shape in your head. Lift all fingers together and land them together.

::tool[A to D changes]{name="changes" a="A" b="D"}

For D to E, let the index lead: it stays on the G string and slides back from fret 2 to fret 1.

::tool[D to E changes]{name="changes" a="D" b="E"}

## Strum it in time

::exercise{id="a-d-quarters"}

Then the full family, A, D, E and back to A. The move from E back to A is the "coming home" sound of the key of A.

::exercise{id="a-d-e-quarters"}

:::warning
Common mistakes:

- Strumming the low E string on A, or the low E and A strings on D.
- Fingers flat on the strings, muting their neighbours.
- Squeezing hard: press just enough for a clean note and keep the thumb relaxed.

:::

**Mastery check:** 30 changes in one minute between A and D.
